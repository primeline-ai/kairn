# Kairn privacy policy

Kairn runs on your computer. It keeps what you choose to save in a local file, `~/.kairn/kairn.db`, and sends nothing to PrimeLine or anyone else. If you turn on the optional semantic recall, Kairn sends the text it embeds to the Ollama server named in `embedding_host`, which is your own computer unless you change it. What your agent recalls is returned to the agent and becomes part of its conversation with the model. Delete `~/.kairn` to remove everything.
